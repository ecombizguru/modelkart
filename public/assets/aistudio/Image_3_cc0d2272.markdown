## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-fashion editorial prestige balanced with institutional transactional trust. Built for leading modeling agencies, luxury fashion houses, art directors, and verified creative professionals, the visual aesthetic draws direct inspiration from archival Vogue layouts, Models.com directory clarity, and contemporary luxury ateliers.

The design movement merges **Editorial Minimalism** with **Structured High-Contrast Precision**:
- **Atmospheric Tension:** Striking contrast between deep obsidian dark tones and luminous ivory paper hues, avoiding cold digital blacks or stark synthetic whites.
- **Editorial Poise:** Ample breathing room, hairline rules, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and asymmetrical grid tension that mirrors printed editorial spreads.
- **Transactional Authority:** Razor-sharp badge systems, verified agency seals, and escrow status indicators rendered with champagne gold leaf accents and crisp low-contrast outlines.

## Colors

The palette operates primarily in a tailored dark mode to mirror private viewing rooms and high-end digital agency portfolios, while maintaining effortless inversion for printable comp cards and contracts.

- **Obsidian Spectrum (`#0A0A0C` to `#1A1A1E`):** Serves as the deep velvet canvas, absorbing visual clutter and allowing full-bleed model photography and studio portfolios to command focus.
- **Ivory Accent (`#FAF8F5` and `#C8C5BD`):** Acts as the primary reading ink and card ground in inverted modules, providing warm, tactile legibility reminiscent of archival art paper.
- **Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37` with `#E8CF7A` and `#997B1E`):** Used strictly for verified talent checkmarks, agency escrow certifications, primary interactive triggers, and elite talent badges. It is deployed sparingly to retain its prestige value.
- **System States:** Escrow balances and contractual milestones utilize calibrated functional tones (`status-escrow` and `status-verified`) subdued against dark surfaces to prevent interface noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between the high-contrast drama of modern fashion editorial headlines and the precise, mechanical clarity of talent logistics:

- **Display & Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** Utilized for talent names, editorial cover headlines, casting brief titles, and marquee feature cards. It injects couture personality and runway elegance.
- **Body & Structural UI (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides razor-sharp, neutral legibility across long-form casting specifications, contractual legalities, and user interactions without competing with photographic assets.
- **Metadata & Badges (JetBrains Mono):** Purposefully deployed for comp-card physical specifications (e.g., `HEIGHT: 179CM / BUST: 84 / WAIST: 60`), escrow tracking hashes, ledger pricing, and verified status badges. All mono label tokens default to uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an asymmetrical 12-column editorial fluid grid that effortlessly transitions between portfolio showcases and dense booking tools.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): 4 columns, single-column talent feeds, fixed compact sheet drawers, 20px edge margins.
  - Tablet (`768px - 1023px`): 8 columns, 2-up comp-card gallery grids, horizontal filter ribbons.
  - Desktop (`≥ 1024px`): 12 columns, max-width container capped at `1600px` for ultra-wide monitors, 32px gutters, multi-pane casting management split views.
- **Editorial Proportions:** Portfolio comp-cards respect traditional print ratios: `4:5` for editorial headshots and `2:3` for full-length looks. Spacing around imagery uses `space-2xl` to establish gallery pacing.
- **Operational Density:** Agency roster dashboards and escrow milestone trackers switch to tighter cell padding (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize visible information density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy blurred drop shadows, utilizing architectural low-contrast hairline borders, dark tonal layering, and fine champagne highlights to construct visual hierarchy:

- **Base Layer:** Canvas background (`#0A0A0C`).
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Modules):** `#121214` framed with a subtle 1px border of `#27272C`.
- **Hover & Active Elevation:** Instead of lifting elements with multi-layered shadows, hovering a comp card or agency tile subtly shifts the border stroke from `#27272C` to `#D4AF37` (40% opacity) accompanied by an internal surface step to `#1A1A1E`.
- **Modals, Flyouts & Booking Drawers:** Background blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) over a 75% dark tint (`rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.75)`), enclosed in a razor-thin 1px perimeter line of `#D4AF37` at 25% opacity.
- **Badge Sheen:** Verified agency seals and VIP badges use a hairline linear metallic gradient stroke (`#E8CF7A` to `#997B1E`) with zero drop shadow.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strict **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)** geometry throughout all foundational structural elements, echoing physical prints, comp-cards, magazine spreads, and architectural lookbooks:

- **Cards, Frames, & Image Containers:** 0px radius. Pure orthogonal precision preserves photographic borders and matches high-fashion print layouts.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** 0px radius. Crisp rectangular buttons emphasize luxury poise, anchored by 1px solid stroke outlines or solid fills.
- **Exceptions (Pills for Badges Only):** Verification status pills, filter toggles, and metadata tags are the only permitted rounded elements in the interface, using pure capsules (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast against the sharp structural grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Runway Gold):** Background `#D4AF37`, text `#0A0A0C`, 0px border radius, sharp hover state transition to `#E8CF7A`. Typographic styling in bold caps with subtle tracking.
- **Secondary (Editorial Wire):** Background transparent, 1px solid `#27272C` border, text `#FAF8F5`. Hover states brighten the border to `#FAF8F5`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#C8C5BD`, no background or border, underline reveals on hover.

### Comp-Card & Talent Tiles
- Aspect ratio fixed to `4:5`.
- Hairline perimeter border (`#27272C`).
- Overlay layer contains talent name in `Bodoni Moda`, with agency affiliation and verification checkmark anchored in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Hover behavior triggers a smooth zoom on photographic assets (scale 1.02) and highlights the physical metric summary (Height, Bust, Waist, Hips, Shoes) in an upward-sliding drawer.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat rectangular field (`#121214`) with a bottom-only 1px hairline border (`#27272C`) or full 1px boundary.
- Active focus state changes border to `#D4AF37` with zero outer ring glow.
- Labels utilize uppercase `label-caps` in `#C8C5BD`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Verified Agency Seal:** Pill geometry, border 1px solid `#D4AF37`, text `#D4AF37`, background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`. Accompanied by a 12px checkmark or laurel icon.
- **Escrow Guarantee:** Pill geometry, border 1px solid `#3B82F6`, text `#93C5FD`, background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08)`.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Pill geometry, inactive state `#1A1A1E` with `#27272C` outline; active state filled with `#FAF8F5` text `#0A0A0C`.

### Casting Brief Review Board & Roster Tables
- Table rows alternate with hairline dividers (`1px solid #1E1E24`).
- Sticky headers use `obsidian-surface` with backdrop blur.
- Escrow milestone steppers use geometric monospaced bullet indicators (`[01] FUNDED`, `[02] IN SHOOT`, `[03] RELEASED`).
